# Q&A Design Rationale: Plain-English Guide for Every Team Member

1. **Why this encoding (`PowerTransformer` + `RY(2 * atan(alpha * x))`):** Pulsar kurtosis and skewness have extreme outlier tails. Fitting Yeo-Johnson strictly on `X_train` tames heavy tails without leaking test statistics, while `2 * atan(alpha * x)` maps any real input smoothly into `(-pi, pi)` so unseen test outliers never wrap modulo `2*pi`.
2. **What the VQC circuit does:** Each input rotates 8 qubits (`<= 8` wires), passes through `L` layers of trainable single-qubit `RY`/`RZ` rotations and `CNOT` entanglers (`none`, `ring`, or domain-paired `bipartite` coupling pulse-profile wires `0-3` to DM-SNR wires `4-7`), and maps Pauli-`Z` expectation values to a pulsar logit trained via minibatch AdamW.
3. **What a quantum fidelity kernel does (`Q2-QuantumKernel`):** Instead of gradient-training circuit weights, a fixed 8-qubit `ZZ` feature map embeds each candidate into a 256-dim quantum state `|psi(x)>`. Pairwise squared overlaps `K(x_i, x_j) = |<psi(x_i) | psi(x_j)>|^2` form a positive-semidefinite Gram matrix solved globally by a convex classical SVM.
4. **Why thresholds are chosen on validation only:** Optimizing the F1/MCC classification threshold on `y_test` leaks test labels and inflates scores. Selecting `tau` strictly on `y_val` keeps `y_test` untouched until final scoring.
5. **What PR-AUC means (and why accuracy fails here):** Only `9.16%` of HTRU2 candidates are true pulsars. Guessing "noise" every time scores `90.83%` accuracy while missing `100%` of pulsars (`0.0917` PR-AUC, `0.0000` MCC). PR-AUC measures precision across all recall levels for the rare positive pulsar class.
6. **What caused v1 VQC collapse & what actually improved accuracy:** In `v1`, single-seed tuning on `seed 100` picked a narrow input scale (`alpha = 0.25`) where `RY(theta)|0>` readout (`<Z> = cos(theta)`, even in `x`) left `2/10` evaluation seeds stalled below `0.70` PR-AUC (`0.7800 +/- 0.1437`). Moving to disjoint 3-seed validation tuning (`seeds 200..202`) already eliminated all collapses on `Ring L=2` (`0/10` collapses, `0.8860`). Single-factor additions on `Ring L=2` (`pi/2` sign offset alone = `0.8854`; 8-wire linear readout alone = `0.8869`) had negligible standalone effect, whereas combining **shallow bipartite wiring (`8` vs. `16` CNOTs) + `pi/2` sign offset + 8-wire linear readout** (`41` params, matching `Tiny-MLP`) lifted PR-AUC to `0.9102 +/- 0.0025`.
7. **Why we do not claim quantum advantage:** At `<= 8` qubits (`2^8 = 256` amplitudes), all circuits are cheaply simulable on a laptop CPU, HTRU2 is nearly linearly separable (`LogReg = 0.9377` full-pool PR-AUC), `ConcentricShell-8D` reflects synthetic `cos(.)` symmetry alignment and a 4-neuron MLP width limit (`h=32` reaches `0.7767`), and `Q2-QuantumKernel` requires `O(N^2)` pairwise circuit runs on physical QPU hardware.
